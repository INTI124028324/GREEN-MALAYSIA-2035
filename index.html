Our vision

Better journeys. Cleaner energy.

Green Malaysia 2035 proposes practical innovations that can help reduce emissions, encourage eco-friendly lifestyles and improve quality of life. The plan brings together public agencies, businesses, educational institutions and local communities.

Why it matters

Challenges we want to address

Malaysia needs solutions that are accessible, affordable and environmentally responsible.

🌫️

Air pollution

Petrol and diesel vehicles release carbon dioxide and other pollutants that affect air quality.

🚗

Traffic congestion

Heavy reliance on private vehicles can increase travel time and fuel consumption.

⚡

Fossil fuel dependence

Coal, natural gas and other fossil fuels contribute to the environmental footprint of energy generation.

The proposed solutions

Six innovations, one shared goal.

Explore the two connected programmes described in our Green Malaysia 2035 proposal.

🚍 GreenMove Malaysia — Sustainable Transportation

🚌
Innovation 01

Solar-Powered Electric Bus Network

Introduce electric buses on selected high-demand routes and install solar panels at suitable bus depots and charging stations. Track passenger use, electricity consumption, costs and emissions.

📱
Innovation 02

GreenMove Rewards App

Reward eligible bus, train, cycling and walking trips with points that may be exchanged for transport discounts or participating-shop vouchers. Include route and arrival information.

🚲
Innovation 03

Green Mobility Hubs

Connect buses, trains, bicycles and other low-carbon travel options at selected stations, universities and commercial centres, with safe bike parking and useful travel information.

☀️ Solar Future Malaysia — Future & Alternative Energy

☀️
Innovation 04

Solar Malaysia Community Programme

Install solar PV panels on suitable schools, universities, government buildings and community halls. Measure energy generation and savings; consider storage where feasible.

♻️
Innovation 05

Waste-to-Energy for Rural Communities

Explore biogas from suitable agricultural residues and organic waste, such as livestock waste and palm-oil processing by-products, with proper safety and environmental monitoring.

🔌
Innovation 06

Solar-Powered EV Charging

Develop solar canopies and EV chargers at suitable malls, universities, offices and public car parks. A digital platform could show charger locations and availability.

How it connects

From clean energy to greener journeys

The two focus areas are designed to support each other, rather than operate separately.

01 · GenerateProduce renewable energy through solar panels and suitable biogas projects.
02 · PowerSupply clean electricity to public facilities, transport depots and charging stations.
03 · MoveSupport electric buses, EV charging and greener travel choices through GreenMove.
04 · ImproveMeasure results and work to reduce fossil fuel use and transport pollution.

Note: Results will depend on feasibility, funding, infrastructure, maintenance and how electricity is generated. These are proposed initiatives, not claims of completed implementation.

Expected outcomes

Benefits for people and the planet

The proposal aims to deliver environmental, economic and social value over time.

🌱

Environmental

Potentially lower transport emissions, improve urban air quality, increase renewable electricity use and turn suitable organic waste into useful energy.

📈

Economic

Potential long-term savings on energy and fuel, alongside job opportunities in solar installation, EV maintenance, software and renewable energy operations.

🤝

Social

More convenient transport choices, public awareness of sustainability and community participation, with attention to affordability and rural access.

A greener future starts together.

Government, businesses, universities and communities all have a role to play.
